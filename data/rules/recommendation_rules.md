# Retail Operational Recommendation Rules

## 1. Important Guidance
- **Advisory Only**: All generated recommendations are strictly advisory guidance for human retail store managers and inventory planners.
- **No Automatic Execution**: The system MUST NEVER automatically execute inventory orders, price updates, or stock transfers.

## 2. Recommendation Strategies

### A. Stock-Out Risk & Low Stock Action Guidelines
- **Predictive Stock-Out Risk / Low Stock**:
  - Issue expedited purchase reorder to supplier.
  - Evaluate store-to-store inventory rebalancing from nearby stores with surplus stock.
  - Temporarily pause marketing promotions on low-stock items until replenishment arrives.

### B. Overstocked & Slow-Moving Action Guidelines
- **Overstock / Slow-Moving Products**:
  - Implement promotional discount or bundle offer with complementary high-demand products.
  - Adjust future reorder parameters and lower target stock levels.
  - Review end-cap product placement or store merchandising to increase visibility.

### C. Sales Spike Action Guidelines
- **Surging Demand / Sales Spike**:
  - Verify safety stock levels and prepare advance replenishment orders.
  - Ensure shelf space allocation matches elevated sales velocity.

### D. Sales Drop Action Guidelines
- **Demand Collapse / Sales Drop**:
  - Investigate product display, stock availability, or price positioning.
  - Check for localized store issues or shifting consumer demand patterns.
